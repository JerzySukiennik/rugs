### 1b. Mistreatment: trigger and visible result

| Rug | Trigger | Visible result |
|---|---|---|
| PP | iron or dryer above ~160 C; sustained heat above 100 C | melt, glaze, shrink; chains degrade above 100 C [S] |
| PP | oily soil, unrinsed detergent | rapid re-soil, dark halo, wicking [S] |
| PET / PA | chlorine (nylon yellows), NOx, fumes under rug | yellow/brown; phenolic (BHT) yellowing under rugs [S] |
| PET / PA | alkaline + heat | dye migration (colour run) [T] |
| Shag | beater bar, rotary, rubbing a stain | broken tips, matting, pooling/watermark [S] |
| Wool | hot water + agitation + soap/alkali | felting, shrink; soap acts as lubricant for felting [S] |
| Wool | temperature shock while damp | accelerates felting [S] |
| Wool | pH above ~8.5 or long alkaline contact | dye bleed in reds, pinks, purples, blues, greens, greys; water alone did not bleed [S] |
| Wool | chlorine | dissolves, thin white/grey patches [S] |
| Wool | heat above iron limit | scorch, char; wool chars rather than melts [S] |
| Silk | alkali, chlorine, sulfuric acid | dissolves or weakens [S]; yellowing [T] |
| Silk | sunlight, perspiration | weakening, yellowing [S] |
| Silk | any water | loses up to 20% strength when wet [S] |
| Viscose (art silk) | any water, wet agitation | swells, distorts, bleeds, shrinks; regular viscose is dry-clean only [S]; yellowing from high-pH residue [S] |
| Sisal / jute | water with slow drying | cellulosic browning: sugars/lignin migrate to yarn tips, yellow-brown [S] |
| Sisal / jute | any wetting, damp backing | mildew, rot, shrink [T]; sisal poorly suited to wet conditions [S] |
| Sisal / jute | dry compound left in pile | browning [S] |
| Cotton | hot wash, hot dryer | shrink, red dye bleed [T] |
| Any | urine left days to months | dye shift, fiber weakening, permanent [S] |

## 3. Soils and stains

**Base spot sequence** [S, IICRC consumer guide]: 1 remove solids; 2 blot dry; 3 blot with clear water (microfibre); 4 mist a few drops of dish soap per cup of water, blot; 5 repeat, blotting between passes; 6 residual colour: 3% H2O2 for hours or several tries (not on wool or natural fibre without test); 7 rinse with clear water once stain is gone; 8 escalate to a pro.

1. **Dry dust, sand, crumbs**
   - Look: loose grit on pile tips; sand scratches fibre.
   - Remove: vacuum (beater bar on PP/PET only, canister on wool/silk); shake or beat outdoors for shag and sisal; dry compound on natural fibre.
   - Don't: wet-scrub grit, it smears into backing.
   - Harder: shag (traps), sisal/jute (grit lodges in weave). UV: no.

2. **Pet hair**
   - Look: loose hairs tangled in loops or shag, matted patches.
   - Remove: rubber brush or squeegee across the pile, rug rake (PP/PET only), canister vac. Dry hair first; wet hair clumps.
   - Don't: beater bar on shag, metal rake on wool/silk.
   - Harder: shag, loop pile. Hair plus moisture makes odour [S]. UV: no.

3. **Dried mud**
   - Look: raised grey-brown flakes or crust; pale mineral rings when dry.
   - Remove: let dry, break up, vacuum; base sequence; alkaline pre-spray on PP/PET, dwell 5-10 min [T]; soft brush, extract, rinse.
   - Don't: hot water (bakes clay in), scrub while wet.
   - Harder: wool (felts if scrubbed wet), sisal (water sets clay). UV: no.

4. **Sticky soda / juice (sugar)**
   - Look: wet sheen; dries to tacky, darker ring with rim.
   - Remove: base sequence; rinse fully, sugar residue draws new soil [S].
   - Don't: leave wet (wicks up), bleach.
   - Harder: wool; food-dye stains such as Kool-Aid match carpet dye [S]. UV: no.

5. **Red wine**
   - Look: flat purple-red, spreads and wicks; can leave a pink ghost [S].
   - Remove: extract big spill first; base sequence; residual 3% H2O2 until gone, hours [S]; clear rinse.
   - Don't: rub; peroxide on wool/natural without test [S]; chlorine.
   - Harder: natural fibre, older synthetics [S]. UV: no.

6. **Coffee / tea (tannin)**
   - Look: brown flat stain, darker rim; milk and sugar make it sticky.
   - Remove: base sequence; acid tannin spotter, pH 2-4 [T], dwell 5 min, blot; residual H2O2; rinse.
   - Don't: ammonia (warned against for coffee and urine) [S]; steam (sets).
   - Harder: wool and tea-dyed rugs, since tea is used to dye wool [S]. UV: no.

7. **Grease / cooking oil**
   - Look: translucent, shiny, flattened patch; spreads; later attracts soil.
   - Remove: absorbent powder 15-30 min [T], vacuum; solvent spotter or degreaser on cloth, dwell 5-10 min [T]; soft brush, extract, rinse.
   - Don't: hot water (sets fat); alkaline degreaser on wool, silk, jute.
   - Harder: PP and PET (oily soil loves them) [T]; wool (test solvents). UV: usually no [T].

8. **Pet urine (fresh and aged)**
   - Look: fresh: darker wet patch, yellow tide ring; aged: invisible, odour, dye shift, stiff fibre [S].
   - Remove: extract with wet/dry vac first [S]; base sequence; enzyme cleaner, pH 6.5-8.5 [S], on synthetics only, dwell 10-15 min to hours, or cover damp 12-24 h for heavy soil [S]; rinse; UV check.
   - Don't: ammonia (remarking) [S]; hot water or steam (sets); let it dry out, so cover with damp white cotton towel [S].
   - Harder: wool (dye damage), jute (fibre weakens), aged urine on any fibre [S].
   - UV: yes. Fresh glows green-yellow. Cleaned areas still glow because phosphorus is bonded to the fibre; this does not mean the clean failed [S]. Blue-white glow is optical brighteners, a false positive [S].

9. **Chewing gum**
   - Look: raised rubbery lump; matted loops.
   - Remove: bone spatula to lift bulk; freeze with aerosol gum freezer such as 3M Gum Remover 8 oz RTU [S]; chip brittle pieces; residue: solvent spotter, dwell 5-10 min [S], blot, rinse.
   - Don't: metal scraper, iron or hair dryer (melts, spreads), hot water.
   - Harder: loop and shag, gum sits between loops [T]. UV: no.

10. **Candle wax**
    - Look: raised waxy crust, translucent or white; coloured dye ring; greasy halo.
    - Remove: blunt scrape; freeze and break off [S]; brown paper over stain, low iron (1 dot wool/silk, 2 dots synthetics), 5-10 s, change paper each pass [S]; solvent spotter 5-10 min for film [S]; cold rinse; blot with white towel [S].
    - Don't: knife; hot iron on wool; overwet.
    - Harder: loop pile, wool (heat limit). UV: usually no [T].

11. **Latex paint**
    - Look: wet: milky, opaque; dry: flat plastic film, stiff.
    - Remove: wet: cold water + dish soap, blot at once [T]; dry: scrape film, latex remover or solvent spotter, dwell 5-10 min, test first [T]; rinse.
    - Don't: hot water (cures film), ammonia.
    - Harder: loop, shag (paint catches loops) [T]. UV: some acrylics with optical brighteners glow blue-white [T].

12. **Oil paint**
    - Look: glossy, thick, brush marks; sticky while wet.
    - Remove: scrape excess; odourless mineral spirits or solvent spotter on cloth, 5 min [T], blot; second pass; cold rinse; dye test first.
    - Don't: acetone or turpentine on synthetics [T]; water (smears).
    - Harder: wool, silk (solvent and dye test), loops. UV: usually no [T].

13. **Ballpoint ink**
    - Look: blue or black dots and lines, halo; migrates when wet.
    - Remove: dab (never rub) with 70% isopropyl on white cloth, change sides, test first [T]; base; rinse. Dye stabilizer does not work on ink [S].
    - Don't: hot water (spreads), rubbing.
    - Harder: wool, silk (dye migration) [T]. UV: most ballpoint dyes no [T].

14. **Mould / mildew**
    - Look: fuzzy or powdery grey, green or black patches; musty smell; often on back or pad side [S].
    - Remove: vacuum outdoors, dry in 24-48 h with airflow [T]; antimicrobial rinse or 3% H2O2 spot; severe: IICRC mould remediation specialist [S].
    - Don't: dry-brush (spreads spores), bleach on wool, roll up damp.
    - Harder: sisal/jute, wool, any wet pad. UV: not reliable [T].

## 5. Professional washing workflow

1. **Intake (10-15 min):** check label, fibre ID, backing, damage, photos. Urine-damaged rugs need a release [S].
2. **UV scan (dark room):** mark urine zones. Fluorescence is normal after a clean [S].
3. **Dye and fibre test:** damp white cloth with hot water or high-pH spotter, rubbed firmly on face and back; colour on cloth = bleed [S]. Then stabilizer test with acetic or citric acid [S]. Cool-water test is milder: 30-60 s on one colour [S].
4. **Dry soil:** vacuum (no beater bar on silk, wool, sisal); shake or beat outdoors for shag [T].
5. **Pre-treatment:** spot per section 3. Pre-spray dwell 5-15 min [T]. Alkaline only on PP/PET/PA. Wool: pH max 8.5, dwell 5 min [T].
6. **Shampoo and agitation:** dilute per label (pre-spray 1:32 [S]). Rotary low speed on PP/PET; soft hand brush on wool [T].
7. **Rinse:** low-pressure wand. PP/PET hot up to 60 C; wool and silk 20-30 C. Repeat until no suds [T].
8. **Extract:** rubber squeegee in direction of pile, then wringer or centrifuge [S]. Sisal and jute skip water entirely (dry compound) [S].
9. **Dry:** 20-25 C, airflow, dehumidifier, rugs flat or on rack, no sun [T]. Hot extraction 6-24 h, thick or heavy wet 24-48 h [S, range]. Slow drying causes browning on jute and cotton [S] and wicking [S]; wait 48 h before wicking fix [S].
10. **Groom:** rake pile one direction; fluff shag by hand [S].
11. **Final inspection:** colour vs baseline; UV check (glow is expected); wicking check after 48 h [S].
12. **Wrap and deliver:** breathable wrap, never plastic on damp rug (mould) [T].

**Facility floor:** one shop describes scrubbing on a wash floor, then rinse and automated wringer [S]; another uses a wash pit designed for oriental rugs [S]. Sloped floor and drain specs were not found [U].

**Time budget:** hands-on for one Persian rug 1-3 h; drying dominates [T].

## 6. Conflicts and gaps

- Wool pH: Prochem WoolSafe products list pH 8-8.5 [S]; Stark says avoid above 8.0 [S]; retail guides say 5.5-7.5 [S]. Game value: 4.5-7 normal, 8-8.5 short dwell only.
- Sisal/jute spot: one retailer suggests 50/50 vinegar-water [S]; IICRC and jute guides say no water [S]. Use dry compound only.
- Heated drying room: 60 C claimed [S]; wool and silk heat limit is 40-45 C or less [S, Wikipedia wool 40-45 C, traditional]. Treat as hazard.
- Enzyme pH: 6.5-8.5 [S]; one unreliable page says acidic for wool. Ignore.
- Traffic-lane pH: 11.0, 11.4, 11.8 across listings [S]; use 11.4.
- HOST pH: 5.2 vs 5.7 on two SDS [S].
- Gaps: silk, sisal, jute, shag weights [U]; rug-plant drying times [U]; drain slope [U]; natural-drying glyph shapes [U]; latex, oil paint, ballpoint procedures [T].
