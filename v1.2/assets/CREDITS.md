- Map files per texture folder: color.jpg, normal.jpg (OpenGL), roughness.jpg, ao.jpg and height.jpg where the source provides them. No AO map for asphalt, cardboard, concrete_floor, leak_stain, metal_painted, plastic.
- leak_stain: color.jpg is black outside the decal. Use opacity.jpg as alphaMap.
- fabric_silk has a flat gold albedo. The satin sheen is in roughness and normal.
